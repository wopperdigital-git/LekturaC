# Lektura - AI Presentation Generator

## What is Lektura?

Lektura is a web-based tool that generates PowerPoint presentations for you. You describe what you want to present, answer a few simple questions, and it creates a complete deck in minutes. Then you can edit, refine, and export it as a PowerPoint file or as a video presentation narrated by your own cloned voice.

---

## The problem it solves

Making a presentation usually takes hours or days. You have to research facts, write slide content, design the layout, and then revise everything multiple times. Most people spend a lot of time on this, or hire someone to do it for them.

Lektura automates the first pass so you can spend your time actually making the deck better, rather than creating it from scratch.

---

## How it works

**Step 1: Answer a few questions**

You tell Lektura five things:
1. What's your topic?
2. How many slides do you want? (1–10, or let it decide)
3. Who's your audience?
4. How much detail?
5. Any specific things to emphasize or leave out? (optional)

**Step 2: It generates your deck**

Lektura researches facts and sources, writes the slide content, designs the layout, and checks the quality. This all happens in seconds before you see anything.

**Step 3: You edit it**

You now have a complete deck that you can edit however you want. Change the text, add or remove slides, rearrange them, drag elements around, or change the theme.

**Step 4: Export and present**

Download the deck as a PowerPoint file, generate a video presentation narrated by your cloned voice, present it right in the app, or share it with others for feedback.

---

## Features

**AI generation**

One call generates the entire deck. It researches facts and sources, structures the content, and checks for quality issues. It won't regenerate your slides endlessly—once you have a deck, you edit it yourself.

**Full editing**

You can edit text on any slide, add or delete slides, rearrange them, drag elements around, resize things, and rotate them. The app also tracks your changes so you can undo if you need to.

**Themes**

Five built-in themes with different looks and backdrops. You can customize typography and colors, and switch between light and dark mode.

**Layouts that adapt**

The app automatically picks a layout for each slide based on what you put in it (text, bullets, numbers, timelines, quotes, etc.). You can also choose a different layout manually if you want variety.

**Speaker notes**

The AI generates speaker notes that you can edit slide by slide. If you want to regenerate notes for a specific slide, you can do that too.

**PowerPoint export**

The files it creates are real PowerPoint files with editable text boxes, not images. They look the same in PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, and Keynote.

**Voice cloning and video narration**

You can clone your own voice and have the app generate a video presentation where your cloned voice narrates the slides. This gives you a professional video presentation without having to record yourself or hire a voice actor.

**Classroom tools for teachers**

Teachers can create a class and invite students with a join code. You can post quizzes and see how students are doing. Students can take quizzes and track their own progress.

**Cloud storage**

All your decks save to the cloud automatically. You can access them from any device, and you just need an email and password to sign in.
